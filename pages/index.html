CONTEXT BEFORE CONCLUSIONS

What these numbers mean.

A historical sample

Each competition contains five sourced championship editions. A row represents one title, including an entire NBA Finals series or a Davis Cup team tie. These are not complete fixture histories or current standings. The five sports are an editorial selection, not a measured popularity ranking.

Keep the denominator in view

Title share is titles divided by sampled editions of the same competition and category. Final conversion is titles divided by final appearances. Neither is a season-wide match win rate. Different years, tournament frequencies and qualification rules prevent a fair global team-strength ranking.

A deeper cricket dataset

The IPL lab uses the original 756 matches and 179,078 delivery records. Shared Python calculations correct mixed dates, keep unresolved matches separate, unify Delhi’s rebrand and Pune’s spelling change, and count only bowler-credited dismissals. Deccan Chargers and Sunrisers Hyderabad remain separate franchises.

Transparent boundaries

World TeamTennis is mixed-gender; the other bundled competitions are men’s. Women-only competitions, complete match/player data outside IPL, predictions, and live results are not included. The public version offers one IPL season or all seasons; use the local Streamlit app for custom championship CSV uploads and multiple-season selections.
